## What it does
A [HandyNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes) plugin that pins the entrances of the **WoW Forever** dungeons and raids on the world map, the continent map and the minimap. Inspired by [HandyNotes: DungeonLocations (Classic)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes_dungeonlocations-classic).

## Features
- 30 dungeon entrances, including the new Forever ones (City of Dalaran, Excavation Site, Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron), and Onyxia's Lair.
- Every wing gets its own pin: Scarlet Monastery, Dire Maul and Stratholme.
- Tooltip with the dungeon name in your game language and its level range (raids: player count).
- Pins on the zone map, the continent map and the minimap, with the game's own dungeon and raid icons.
- Same entrances as [Dungeon Quest Atlas Forever](https://github.com/Pirson-s-Addons/DungeonQuestAtlas).
- Translated into 20 languages.

## Installation
1. Download the zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/Pirson-s-Addons/HandyNotes_DungeonLocations-Forever/releases/latest).
2. Extract the `HandyNotes_DungeonLocations-Forever` folder into `World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`.
3. Restart WoW and enable the addon.

## Usage
- Open the map: the entrances show up as dungeon and raid icons.
- No slash commands or options of its own; enable or disable it in the HandyNotes options.

## Notes
- **Requires [HandyNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes).**
- WoW Forever only.
